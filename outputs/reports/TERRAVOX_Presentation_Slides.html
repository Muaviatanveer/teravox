02 · PIPELINE A: RADAR BACKSCATTER MATHEMATICS

Radiometric Backscatter Change (Δγ⁰) & Specular Flood Physics

1. RTC Decibel Backscatter Change

Using NISAR L2 GCOV Radiometrically Terrain-Corrected (RTC) linear power (HHHH):

$$\gamma^0_{\mathrm{dB}} = 10\log_{10}(\gamma^0_{\mathrm{linear}}), \quad \Delta\gamma^0_{\mathrm{dB}} = \gamma^0_{\mathrm{dB}, t_2} - \gamma^0_{\mathrm{dB}, t_1}$$

2. UARI Log-Speckle Uncertainty

$$\sigma_{\Delta\gamma^0} = \frac{10}{\ln 10}\sqrt{\frac{1}{\mathrm{ENL}_1} + \frac{1}{\mathrm{ENL}_2}} \approx \pm 2.68\text{ dB}$$

3. Specular Reflection Physics

• Dry Farmland (t₁): Rough soil scatters radar diffusely back to sensor (High return: -10 dB).

• Standing Floodwater (t₂): Smooth water surface acts like a flat mirror, reflecting radar pulses away into space.

• Signal Drop: Pixel turns pitch-black (-18 dB). Change of -8 dB confirms standing water.

$$\text{Flood Mask: } \Delta\gamma^0 \le -3.0\text{ dB} \quad \text{and} \quad \gamma^0_{t_2} \le -14.0\text{ dB}$$
03 · PIPELINE B: INTERFEROMETRIC PHASE MATHEMATICS

InSAR Phase Unwrapping (Δφ) & Millimeter Line-of-Sight Displacement

1. Exact Radar Wavelength Calculation

Reading exact center frequency $f_0 = 1.2575\text{ GHz}$ from NISAR metadata:

$$\lambda = \frac{c}{f_0} = \frac{2.9979 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}{1.2575 \times 10^9\text{ Hz}} = 0.2384\text{ m} = 238.4\text{ mm}$$

2. Ionospheric Delay Correction

$$\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{corrected}} = \Delta\phi_{\mathrm{unwrapped}} - \phi_{\mathrm{ionosphere}}$$

3. Two-Way Phase-to-LOS Displacement

$$d_{\mathrm{LOS}} = -\frac{\lambda}{4\pi}\,\Delta\phi_{\mathrm{corrected}} \times 1000 \quad (\text{in mm})$$

• Sign Convention: Negative displacement ($-322.5\text{ mm}$) indicates movement away from satellite (valley floor subsidence).

• Each $2\pi$ fringe cycle equals $\lambda/2 = 119.2\text{ mm}$ of range change.

04 · RESEARCH CONTRIBUTIONS

UARI (Cramér-Rao Bounds) & APOF (Phase–Offset Fusion)

UARI: Theoretical Cramér-Rao Bound (CRLB)

Interferometric phase noise is governed by coherence magnitude $\gamma$ and looks $N_L \approx 36$:

$$\sigma_{\phi}^2 = \frac{1 - \gamma^2}{2 \cdot N_L \cdot \gamma^2} \quad (\text{rad}^2), \quad \sigma_{d_{\mathrm{LOS}}} = \left|-\frac{\lambda}{4\pi}\right|\sqrt{\sigma_{\phi}^2 + \sigma_{\mathrm{iono}}^2} \times 1000$$

Verified result in Quetta: $\mathbf{-322.5\text{ mm} \pm 24.7\text{ mm}}$ explicit uncertainty.

APOF: Adaptive Phase–Offset Fusion

$$\hat{d} = \frac{w_p\,d_p + w_o\,d_o}{w_p + w_o}, \quad w_p = \frac{\gamma^2}{\sigma_p^2 + \epsilon}, \quad w_o = \frac{\rho^2}{\sigma_o^2 + \epsilon}$$

Cross-method disagreement $D_{\mathrm{APOF}} = |d_p - d_o|$ is preserved to flag phase unwrapping errors rather than blindly averaging conflicting signals.
